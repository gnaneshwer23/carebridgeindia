Clarity before commitment

The questions that matter.

How will providers be assessed?

The proposed service will check clinician registration, hospital credentials, procedure-specific information and care responsibilities before listing a partner. No providers have been verified or onboarded in this prototype.

What should a complete quote include?

Clinical fees, hospital stay, tests, implants where applicable, rehabilitation, exclusions, quote validity and arrangements for unexpected costs. Travel and accommodation should be shown separately.

Who will provide aftercare?

A live service must establish named contacts and follow-up responsibilities before booking. UK aftercare arrangements, costs and escalation routes must be agreed; NHS-funded routine aftercare should not be assumed.

Can I book or upload medical records here?

This is an interactive concept. It does not accept bookings, payments or medical records. Treatment decisions require a qualified clinician, and individual suitability for overseas care must be assessed.
